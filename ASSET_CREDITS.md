The text files are mechanical extracts of the supplied DOCX transcriptions. Extraction added stable paragraph markers, retained the source wording, and introduced no new historical interpretation. The unchanged DOCX files remain available for inspection.

Window designers, makers, memorial donors, and the photographer are different kinds of attribution. Historical artist and donor claims belong in the evidence-backed content catalog; they do not establish the creator of a modern photograph or coloring design. The current source checkpoint leaves those media assessments pending.

When a supported credit or source-specific term becomes available, update this record, the relevant provenance/content entry, and any public or print credit that uses it. Preserve the distinction between an established fact and an unresolved credit.
